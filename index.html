Preparing an archive assessment

Agree the workload and evidence before arranging a capacity and search-performance assessment.

Reference ML-017 · Revision 1.0 · Customer engineering

An import check and a capacity assessment answer different questions. Import checks establish whether records and their metadata are accepted correctly. A capacity assessment measures how the configured archive behaves with a representative document collection and search workload.

Workload information

The assessment brief should cover the following inputs. Open items can be discussed at the preparation meeting.

InputDetails to agree
Document collectionTarget record count, typical document size and page count, languages, and the mix of scanned and text-bearing PDFs.
Import statusAccepted and rejected record counts, unresolved metadata issues, and which records will be available in the assessment index.
Search activityRepresentative searches, the expected number of simultaneous users, and the intended request rate.
EnvironmentArchive configuration, allocated infrastructure, software versions, and any import or maintenance activity running alongside the searches.
MeasurementAssessment duration, warm-up, cold or warm cache conditions, latency target, error threshold, and the evidence to retain.
CoordinationProposed assessment window and time zone, technical contacts, and the support activities required from Meridian.

Evidence from the run

The results should identify the workload and environment used, the number of completed and failed requests, and the measurement interval. Include median and 95th-percentile search latency, with errors and timeouts reported separately.

Record any excluded documents or searches and explain the reason. Keep results from different record volumes, concurrency levels and cache conditions distinguishable. A small single-client run is useful as a baseline; the agreed larger workload needs its own measurements.

Arranging support

Send the assessment brief and proposed window to the delivery contact. Meridian confirms support availability and scope in writing once the workload and required activities are understood. Support can include connector diagnostics, assessment preparation and interpretation of results.

The customer team provides the assessment environment and makes its release and acceptance decisions using the agreed criteria and recorded results.
